The families

Nine family lines researched so far, each with its home town and story.

Rosenblum

Iasi, then Galati

Meir and Tauba Rosenblum of Iasi. Their son Peretz (Peritz), born 1879 in Iasi, was a carpenter in Galati; Meir was already dead when Peretz married Liza Finkelstein in Galati on 1 July 1906. Children on record: Anna-Rose 1907, Fani 1909, Meir 1911 (died as a baby), Philip 1912 and Eva 1914. Yehuda was born in Galati on 21 January 1925 (his birth certificate names Peritz and Leia as parents; the family tree says 1924) and died in 2007. His sisters Sali and Klara come from the family tree - their birth records have not been found yet. Yehuda was held in Cyprus (Camp 63, Jan 1948) and reached Haifa on 4 Feb 1949.

Finkelstein

Galati

Banim and Rifka Finkelstein of Galati were both alive and gave their consent in person at Liza's wedding in 1906; Banim's trade reads 'precupet' (a small trader, partly legible). Liza's age was estimated by the clerk in 1906 (18, so '1888'), but her 1909 and 1914 records put her birth around 1880. Possible brothers Leib (1880) and Yanko (1882) come from earlier research notes.

Maidanek

Vicovu de Sus / Horodnic, Bukovina

The earliest is Abraham-Yaakov, who died in Radautz in 1860 aged 67. Yoel and Taube had Hersch (Zvi), born 1851, died 1928. Hersch and Jitte-Nesie Herer had Abraham (b.1871), Mali (1893) and Moses (1886). Abraham and Chaje Odel Jurgrau had six children between 1896 and 1905, including Mendel (Emil, b.1898), who married Rosa Kurzberg in 1922. Their daughters: Dontzia (1924), Miriam 'Miza' (1926) and Yeti (Yehudit, c.1931); a son Josef (1923) died as a baby. In October 1941 the family was deported to Transnistria: Abraham, Chaje Odel, Emil, his brothers Chaim and Joel, his sister Anna, his aunt Mali and her husband Arie Priling all died there. Rosa, Miza and Yeti survived, and so did Dontzia (the family tree has her dying in Caracas in 2006); Duvid (b.1927) died in Transnistria in July 1942 (the family tree). The sawmill in Vicovu de Sus belonged to a second Maidanek family, the 'Maidanek Brothers' Adolf and Josef - probably sons of Moses and Marie of Oberwikow. Marie was the daughter of David Maidanek of Radautz, who was probably a brother of Joel, so the sawmill brothers were very probably Emil's cousins (second cousins of his father Abraham) - probable, not proven.

Jurgrau

Oberhoradnik (Horodnic de Sus)

Salomon (Shlomo) Jurgrau, a tavern keeper, son of Chaim and Beile, married Chane Fride Besner of Campulung; their children were registered from 1858 and legitimized by the civil marriage of 29 Dec 1874. Their daughter Chaje Odel married Abraham Maidanek in 1901.

Besner

Campulung / Radautz

Chane Fride Besner (c.1836), daughter of Alter and Marjem Besner of Campulung - known so far only from the 1874 marriage index.

Kurzberg

Oberwikow (Vicovu de Sus)

Noach-Yehuda Kurzberg (1844-1907) married Etil-Golda (Ettie Golde) Laufer in 1881; their son Meir-Zeev married Henia Lea Rafoul in 1901 and had ten children between 1894 and 1912, Rosa (1903) among them. Rosa's brother Aharon, his wife Beilchi and their four children were murdered in 1942 - they are the names on the memorial plaque on Meir-Zeev's grave. Henia Lea died in Transnistria in 1942.

Laufer

Oberwikow

Mordechai-Leib and Lea Laufer, parents of Etil-Golda; Abraham Chaim and Devora Laufer, parents of Resi-Ruchel. Mostly from gravestone inscriptions - not yet checked against records.

Rafoul / Raful

Radautz

Schaje Moshe Rafoul (Raful/Reful) and Mariasya Horer had Henia Lea (1872) and at least five more children, 1871-1879. Henia's own Page of Testimony (1957) names her parents Yeshayahu Moshe and Miriam.

Horer / Herer

Radautz

Moshe-Mordechai (Mortko Moshe) Horer married Resi-Ruchel Laufer in 1876; their daughter Mariasya (d.1894) was Henia Lea's mother. Jitte-Nesie Herer (Hersch Maidanek's wife) was a daughter of Menachem-Mendil Herer.

Herer

Radautz

Jitte-Nesie (Yuta-Nesya) Herer, daughter of Menachem-Mendil, wife of Hersch Maidanek; died 1928.
